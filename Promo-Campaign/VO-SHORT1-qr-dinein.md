# VO — Short 1: QR Dine-In (Hinglish)

Total ~70s. Pace: one line per take segment. TTS voice: warm, unhurried.

1. [qr-welcome-menu, 0-8s]
"Restaurant mein ghusne ke saath hi — koi app download nahi, koi wait nahi.
Table ka QR scan karo, START ORDERING dabao — poora menu saamne."

2. [qr-welcome-menu scroll, 8-18s]
"Photo wala menu, category pills, combo offers — jo pasand aaye, tap karo.
Size chuno — Small ya Medium — aur checkout se pehle bill live dikhta hai."

3. [qr-order customize→cart, 18-35s]
"Cheese Pizza select kiya, Medium size, cart mein add.
Naam, mobile number, guests — bas. Coupon hai? Code lagao, discount turant.
PLACE ORDER — aur order seedha kitchen mein."

4. [qr-order status, 35-50s]
"Ab dekho live status — Order Received, Preparing, Ready, Served.
Timer chal raha hai, timeline ban rahi hai. 'Mera khana kahan hai?' — ye sawaal khatam.
Aur beech mein kuch aur chahiye? Order More Items — ek aur pizza, bina waiter ko bulaye."

5. [help/bill — shoot pending]
"Paani chahiye? Waiter? Bill? Ek tap — seedha staff ke paas, table number ke saath.
Bill tabhi jab saara khana serve ho chuka ho — system khud check karta hai."

6. [qr-promos, 50-70s]
"Aur jaate-jaate — Google pe review, Instagram pe follow, WhatsApp pe chat.
Ek table sirf ek bill nahi — agli visit ki booking hai. Ye hai QR table ordering —
tez turnover, zero extra staff, aur har customer se dobara milne ka rasta."
