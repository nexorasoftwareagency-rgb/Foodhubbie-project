Common questions

What software does a cafe need?

A cafe needs four things working together: a menu customers can order from, a screen where the barista sees the ticket, a billing counter, and a way to handle delivery when offered. FoodHubbie bundles all four — QR table ordering, kitchen display, POS billing and rider dispatch — in one realtime system at ₹1–5 per order.

Does it work for cloud kitchens without a dine-in area?

Yes. Dine-in QR is optional — cloud kitchens use the same system for kitchen display, POS billing, delivery riders and WhatsApp ordering, and pay per order only when orders actually come in.

Can I run multiple outlets from one login?

Yes — every location is an outlet under one login, with its own menu, riders, staff and live reports. Owners running a cafe and a restaurant together manage both from the same dashboard.

Is it suitable for a small single-counter restaurant?

Yes. At the ₹1 per POS order tier, a small quick-service restaurant fills orders at the counter, watches them on the kitchen screen, and tracks daily revenue in one CSV — no monthly fee to justify even in a slow month.
