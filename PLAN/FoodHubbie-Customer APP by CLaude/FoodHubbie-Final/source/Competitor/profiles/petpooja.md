## Features

Read first-hand from `petpooja.com/pricing` **[site]**, 2026-09-26. Three plan groups, each with
tiers - the list below is what they say is included, **not tested**:

**Base (INR 12,000):** Cloud Billing - Customized/Dynamic Reporting - Inventory - Menu Management
- **90+ Reports** - **Food Aggregator Integrations** - Unlimited Users & Terminals - Purchase
Manager (Hyperpure, Swiggy Assure, DeliverIT) - Smart-Stock Manager - **AI Agent, unlimited
queries** - Installation & Training (online) - Support 24x7 (online).

**Operations Manager Growth -> Scale (INR 20,000 / 30,000):** Captain App - Token Management -
**Zomato/Swiggy Reconciliation** - Tally - **Scan & QR Order** - Waiter Calling - **Kitchen
Display System** - API Integration - SAP/ERP - Call Centre; *Scale adds* Table Reservation Manager,
Kiosk Software, E-Invoice (1,000 credits), 2 installation visits.

**Marketing Manager Core -> Growth -> Scale (INR 20,000 / 30,000 / 40,000):** CRM + Loyalty -
Virtual Wallet - Feedback App/QR - **Reputation Management** - E-Bill - AI Marketing Agent -
**WABA** (4,000 / 8,000 / 10,000 credits); *Growth adds* My Website/OOW, Digital Display;
*Scale adds* Meta Ads or 2 influencers.

> **Two facts worth carrying into a sales conversation:** **QR ordering and KDS are NOT in the
> Base plan** - both start at INR 20,000/year **[site]**. And their own page advertises **40% of
> online orders processed on Zomato & Swiggy** and **1,50,000+ clients served globally** [site].

Runs on iOS, Windows and Android. Supports A4/A5, WiFi, LAN, USB, dot-matrix, laser, label and
Bluetooth printers. Offers data migration from a previous POS. **[web]** (not re-verified against
the current page).

## What this means for us

**[assessment]**

- **They are the default choice** in our market. Beating them on brand is not realistic; beating
  them on WhatsApp and delivery ownership is.
- **Their differentiators we do not have:** aggregator integration, physical field support,
  published price credibility, hundreds of reviews.
- **Our differentiators they do not advertise:** WhatsApp-native ordering, own rider fleet with
  OTP dispatch and settlement, and a per-order pricing model (INR 1/customer) far below their
  floor.
- **Lead with tiering, not price.** Their **QR ordering and KDS are not in the Base plan** - both
  start at INR 20,000/year **[site]**. Ours are in the base product. That argument does not depend
  on the billing period being annual, so it survives even if this inference is wrong.
- **Their public rate card is a weapon for them - and for us.** Our pricing is published too;
  `../PRICING.md` shows three of the five largest vendors publish nothing at all.
