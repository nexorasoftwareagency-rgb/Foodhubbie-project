## 3. Reviews and case studies (honest only)

Never buy reviews, never invent customer quotes, never publish an unnamed "restaurant owner" as if
it were a testimonial.

**Ask a real customer (WhatsApp message to a restaurant you actually serve):**

> Hi <name>, we run FoodHubbie for <their outlet> since <month>. Would you be comfortable
> writing 3–4 lines about how it works for you — what changed since you started, and what you
> would tell another owner? We may publish it on our site with your outlet name, or anonymously if
> you prefer. Happy to send you the page first.

**Where to collect them**
- Google reviews → link to the GBP listing (GBP reviews also feed local pack ranking)
- Capterra / G2 → invite the same customer, same words, no pressure
- Industry press / blogs → pitch the same story with real numbers (orders per day, staff hours
  saved, commission saved). Numbers beat adjectives.

**Case-study template** (one page each, on the site, linked from the homepage later):
`Outlet name + city · setup date · what they used before (paper KOT, aggregator, monthly POS) ·
the problem in one line · what FoodHubbie changed (with a number) · what it costs them per month ·
one quote with a name and outlet.`
