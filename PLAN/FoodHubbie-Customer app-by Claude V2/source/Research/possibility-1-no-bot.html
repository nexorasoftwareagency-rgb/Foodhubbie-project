·Reading this outcome

  • Floor of the whole analysis. Every other possibility is this number + server + bot Firebase + Meta.
  • ₹/order falls 4× from 10→100 orders/day (₹0.39→₹0.09): free RTDB/Hosting pools + zero fixed server.
  • Images live in Firebase Storage by default — ~75 KB/scan payload, never base64 in RTDB (RTDB-image mode would add ₹259–22,786/mo at scale).
  • The customer-first chat channel disappears — customers can't message to order; ordering is QR menu + POS only.
  • 1 restaurant = ₹0/mo; first bill appears at ~4 busy restaurants (≈₹49) once the shared 10 GB RTDB pool is exhausted.
Who this is for
Operations that don't want WhatsApp ordering yet, or want the cheapest possible floor while piloting QR ordering. Zero servers, zero Meta risk, zero message bills — and the easiest path to re-enable later (add EC2 + bot back).
