·Reading this outcome

  • Meta is the bill. It is 88% of the worst cell and it grows linearly with orders — Firebase and server barely move with volume.
  • No free tier → exactly linear: every delivery order costs 8 × ₹0.115 = ₹0.92 service+utility + ₹0.09 promos ≈ ₹1.01 in Meta fees. 10 orders/day = ₹302/mo per outlet → ₹3,018 at 100 orders/day.
  • ₹/order falls with volume but never reaches Possibility 1: at 100 rest it's ₹2.19 (10 orders) → ₹1.15 (100 orders), while no-bot is ₹0.68→₹0.09 — Meta's share grows from ~3× to ~13× the no-bot cost per order.
  • Levers ranked: --mktg=0 (drop promos, −₹0.09/order) → Baileys/no-bot if the Meta bill can't be justified.
