WhatsApp marketing questions, answered plainly

Can I message customers who never ordered from me?

No. A campaign only targets customers whose promotional consent is recorded against their profile — usually collected when they order or when they agree to offers. Imported numbers without consent are not a legitimate audience and are not something the campaign builder creates.

How many messages can one campaign send?

A campaign is capped at 300 recipients. Sends are paced rather than blasted in one burst, and a campaign can be paused or stopped entirely from the dashboard while it runs.

What happens when a customer replies STOP?

STOP is honoured immediately and that customer is removed from promotional sends. Transactional messages about an order already in progress are separate and continue.

Do I need approved templates to send an offer?

Yes. Marketing messages outside an open conversation must use a template approved by Meta. The composer starts from ready-made shells — offers, discounts, festive and win-back, in English and Hinglish — and fills in the offer details, so nothing is written from scratch.

What does a campaign cost?

Promotional messages are ₹1 each on the WhatsApp Official plans. WhatsApp's own conversation charges for the official API apply separately, and campaigns run only on the official connection — promotional sending is disabled on unofficial connections.
