Customer database questions, answered plainly

Do I have to import my customer list?

No. The book builds itself from orders — each order records the phone, name and amount, and repeat orders are grouped under the same customer with a running visit count and lifetime spend. An existing list can be exported, but a fresh outlet needs no import to start.

What can I see about a customer?

Number of orders, lifetime spend, average order value and the order history, reached from the customer's row. Search works by name or phone number.

Is there a loyalty points system?

No. There are no points or wallet balances. Repeat business is encouraged with first-order offers, coupons and win-back campaigns instead, and each customer records whether they agreed to promotional messages.

Who can see phone numbers?

Only signed-in staff of the outlet. Customer contact details are stored separately from publicly readable order and table data, so a QR menu visitor cannot read the customer book.
