One login, separate businesses

The outlet switcher in the top bar is the whole idea: every screen — orders, tables, menu, inventory, customers, riders, reports — redraws for the outlet you selected. Staff at the branch keep their own accounts; you keep the view from the top.

What is shared, what is separate

🏪
Separate by default

Menu and prices, tables and their QR codes, inventory, customers, riders, staff and reports all belong to one outlet.

🔑
Your login spans them

One owner account switches between outlets. No second subscription, no separate dashboard to keep open.

👥
Staff stay in their branch

Accounts are created per outlet, so a manager or rider sees the location they were added to.

📱
Menus can differ

A cafe's menu and a restaurant's menu are separate catalogues — different items, prices and availability, on their own QR codes.

🛵
Riders per location

Each branch assigns from its own rider list, with its own delivery ledger, because a rider cannot deliver for two kitchens at once.

🧾
Usage per outlet

Orders are counted where they are taken, so the bill for a quiet branch is not inflated by a busy one.

Multi-outlet questions, answered plainly

Do I pay a licence fee for each outlet?

No. There is no per-location licence. Each outlet's orders are billed on their own per-order usage, so a second outlet on the same login adds only the orders it actually takes.

Is each outlet's data separate?

Yes. Menus, tables, QR codes, orders, customers, staff, riders and reports belong to a specific outlet. Switching outlets switches the data set, and a staff account only reaches the outlets it was created for.

Can an outlet manager see the other outlets?

Only if you give them access. Staff accounts are created per outlet, so a manager at one branch works inside that branch unless the owner adds them to another.

Is there a combined view across all outlets?

Each outlet's dashboard, reports and billing are its own. Comparing outlets means switching between them rather than a single merged revenue number, and there is no consolidated multi-outlet report today.
