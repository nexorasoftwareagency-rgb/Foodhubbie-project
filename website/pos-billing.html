POS questions, answered plainly

Does it run on a normal laptop or tablet?

Yes. The POS is a browser app — any laptop, tablet or desktop with a modern browser can be the cashier screen, and phones can run it as a backup.

Can I use it without internet?

No. FoodHubbie is a live cloud system, so the POS, kitchen display and rider app need an internet connection; there is no offline-first mode.

What is POS software in a restaurant?

Restaurant POS (point of sale) software is the screen your cashier uses to take orders, calculate bills, track which tables owe money, and close the day. FoodHubbie's POS goes further: the same screen receives QR table orders and delivery orders automatically — the cashier bills what the kitchen is already cooking, instead of re-entering it.

How much does restaurant POS software cost in India?

Most Indian POS vendors charge ₹1,500–5,000 per month. FoodHubbie charges a flat ₹1–5 per order (by plan) plus a one-time ₹500 setup — quiet days cost almost nothing, and the free trial covers every feature.

Does the POS work with QR code table orders?

Yes — that is the core of FoodHubbie. Customers order from the table QR, the order lands in the kitchen and the cashier's POS with the table number pre-attached. The cashier never re-types an order a customer already placed.

Can I split a table's bill?

Yes. A table session can hold multiple bill groups. Customers request their own bill from their phone, the cashier sees exactly which items belong to which group and accepts or rejects before closing — no shorthand math on paper.

What reports does the POS give me?

Item-level sales, revenue by channel (dine-in / QR / delivery), discounts used, rider delivery performance, and a one-click CSV export of any day's billing — all under Reports in the admin dashboard.

What an Indian restaurant bill actually needs

A bill is not just the sum of the items. The cashier has to get the tax right, apply the discount that was promised, split it three ways for a group, and still close the day with a total that matches the drawer. The POS is built around those four jobs.

GST and service charge

Tax is calculated on the net after discounts, and service charge stays a separate, visible line — so the bill matches what your accountant expects.

Discounts that actually apply

Global, category, coupon and new-customer discounts are scored by the same engine whether the order came from the counter, a table QR or WhatsApp. No manual override.

Split bills without shorthand

A table session can hold several bill groups. Each group's items stay attached to it, and the cashier accepts or rejects a customer's bill request before closing.

A day-close you can trust

Revenue by channel, discounts used, cancelled orders excluded and a CSV export of the day — numbers that reconcile instead of being re-typed into a notebook.

The kitchen side of the same order is covered in kitchen display (KDS); the table side is in QR ordering.
