Rider questions, answered plainly

How do restaurants manage their delivery riders?

FoodHubbie gives the restaurant a rider roster where every rider has their own login to a personal rider app. Delivery orders land on the owner's dashboard with an Assign button; assigning pushes the job straight to that rider's phone, and the customer gets their name and number on WhatsApp. Status flows assign → pickup → delivered, live for everyone.

Can I use freelance or part-time riders instead of staff?

Yes — that's how FoodHubbie is designed. There is no payroll module you must feed; a freelance rider just needs a phone number to get a login. You pay per delivery from the COD collected, settle daily, and the rider ledger keeps the count.

What do customers see when a rider is assigned?

The customer gets a WhatsApp message with the rider's name and phone number, and an OTP-based handover message when the order goes out for delivery — so nobody calls the restaurant asking "where is my order?".

How are delivery payments calculated?

Per delivery, not per month. Each completed delivery is logged against the rider in the ledger; you decide the per-delivery rate and settle. COD collections stay with the rider and are tracked until the ledger clears.
