How FoodHubbie ERP Works: From QR Scan to Bill

Here's what actually happens when a customer scans a QR code, dines in, or orders delivery — and how your team and riders use FoodHubbie every day.

🙂

Customers

Scan, browse, order, pay, and track — all from their own phone. No app download.

👨‍🍳

Kitchen

Orders appear on the kitchen screen instantly. Cook, mark ready, done.

🛵

Riders

Go online, get assigned deliveries, follow the flow, and get paid per delivery.

🧑‍💼

Owner / Staff

One dashboard for orders, tables, menu, inventory, promotions, and money.

📱
