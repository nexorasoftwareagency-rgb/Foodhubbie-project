Before you decide

What is the cheapest restaurant POS software in India?

The cheapest workable option is per-order pricing: FoodHubbie charges a flat ₹1–5 per order (₹1 for counter POS, ₹2 for QR table ordering, ₹5 for WhatsApp ordering) plus a one-time ₹500 setup. Monthly-fee POS vendors typically charge ₹1,500–5,000 per month regardless of volume, so on quiet months per-order costs less.

Is FoodHubbie a good alternative to Petpooja or Posist?

It depends on size. For a single outlet up to a handful of outlets, FoodHubbie covers ordering (QR, WhatsApp, counter), kitchen display, billing, riders, inventory and reports at per-order pricing. Large multi-city chains that need deep enterprise integrations may still prefer enterprise suites like Posist (Restroworks) — we say so plainly in the table above.

Does FoodHubbie include delivery rider management?

Yes — every rider (staff or freelance) gets a personal app login, orders are assigned with one tap, customers get the rider's name and number on WhatsApp, and deliveries are logged in a per-rider ledger. In most monthly-fee systems this is a paid add-on module.

Do I need to buy POS hardware?

No. FoodHubbie runs in the browser and on phones you already own — the cashier screen, kitchen display and owner dashboard all run on ordinary devices. No terminals, printers or installation visits are required.
