A day, a week, the month to date, or a custom start and end date. The outlet filter applies the same way as everywhere else in the dashboard.

Reporting questions, answered plainly

Which reports does FoodHubbie give a restaurant?

A revenue report for any date range with total revenue, order count, average order value and unique customers plus a previous-period comparison; an order-level detail table; rider performance with orders and earnings; discount usage; expenses; and WhatsApp message usage per day.

Can I export the report for my accountant?

Yes. The revenue report exports to Excel for the spreadsheet and to PDF for a signed copy, both covering the selected date range and outlet. The rider report exports the same way.

Does it compare this week to last week?

Yes. The revenue report fetches the previous period of equal length, so a date range shows the change in revenue, orders and average ticket rather than a number in isolation.

Are cancelled orders counted in revenue?

No. Cancelled orders are excluded from revenue, KPI totals and CSV exports, so the report matches what was actually billed.
