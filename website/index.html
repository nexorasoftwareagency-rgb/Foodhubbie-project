How it works

Three steps. Zero training.

Your staff already know how to run a restaurant. We just removed the paper, the shouting and the phone tag.

Step 1

Print the QR code

Stick a QR code on every table. Customers open your menu on their phone, customize their order, and pay — no waiter needed.

Step 2

Kitchen sees it instantly

Orders land on the Kitchen Display Screen in real time. Pending → Placed → Prepared → Served. No tickets lost, no callouts missed.

Step 3

Riders get dispatched

Delivery orders auto-assign to the nearest free rider. Customers track them live on a map. OTP confirms the handoff.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Straight answers about price, setup and how the system runs day to day.

How much does restaurant management software cost in India?

FoodHubbie charges a flat per-order fee instead of commission: QR table orders ₹2, POS counter sales ₹1, official WhatsApp orders ₹5 (or 1% of the order value), and promo messages ₹1 each. One-time setup is ₹500, non-refundable and adjusted in your first bill. No monthly lock-in.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Customers scan a table QR code and order straight from their phone browser. Riders use the Rider App; your staff uses the Admin Dashboard. Nothing for customers to install.

How do my riders get delivery orders?

The system auto-assigns ready orders to the nearest free rider. The rider picks up, follows the delivery flow, and hands over against a customer OTP. You track every delivery live on a map.

Is WhatsApp ordering safe for my number?

New restaurants ship on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API. Promotional broadcasts only go to customers who opted in, use Meta-approved templates, are paced rather than blasted, honour STOP, and refuse to start on unofficial connections.

Can I run multiple outlets from one login?

Yes. Multiple cafes, restaurants and locations run as outlets under one login with per-outlet switching, menus, riders and reports.

How do I start?

Start a free trial on WhatsApp or register your restaurant on this site. Our team reviews applications within 24 hours; once approved you log in to your dashboard and we help you print table QR codes and go live.
