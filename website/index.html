FAQ

Questions owners ask us

Straight answers about price, setup and how the system runs day to day.

How much does restaurant management software cost in India?

FoodHubbie charges a flat per-order fee instead of commission: QR table orders ₹2, POS counter sales ₹1, official WhatsApp orders ₹5 (or 1% of sales), and promo messages ₹1 each. One-time setup is ₹500, non-refundable and adjusted in your first bill. No monthly lock-in.

Do my customers need to download an app?

No. Customers scan a table QR code and order straight from their phone browser — dine-in, takeaway and delivery. Riders use the Rider App; your staff uses the Admin Dashboard. Nothing for customers to install.

How do my riders get delivery orders?

The system auto-assigns ready orders to the nearest free rider. The rider picks up, follows the delivery flow, and hands over against a customer OTP. You track every delivery live on a map and settle earnings from the rider wallet.

Is WhatsApp ordering safe for my number?

Yes — new restaurants ship on the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API: zero ban risk and promo-ready. Promotional broadcasts are paced, STOP is always honored, and campaigns refuse to start on unsafe connections.

Can I run multiple outlets — cafe and restaurant — from one login?

Yes. Multiple cafes, restaurants and locations run as outlets under one login with per-outlet switching, menus, riders and reports.

How do I start?

Start a free trial on WhatsApp or register your restaurant on this site. Our team reviews applications within 24 hours; once approved you log in to your dashboard and we help you print table QR codes and go live.
