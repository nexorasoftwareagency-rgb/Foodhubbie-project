How It Works

Three steps. Zero training.

Your staff already know how to run a pizzeria. We just removed the paper, the shouting, and the phone tag.

1

Print the QR code

Stick a QR code on every table. Customers open your menu on their phone, customize their pizza, and order — no waiter needed.

2

Kitchen sees it instantly

Orders land on the Kitchen Display Screen in real time. Pending → Placed → Prepared → Served. No tickets lost, no callouts missed.

3

Riders get dispatched

Delivery orders auto-assign to the nearest free rider. Customers track them live on a map. OTP confirms the handoff.

QR Table Ordering

Zero-Waiter QR Table Ordering

Every table gets a QR code. Customers order from their seat, the kitchen fires instantly, and bills stay organized — even when a group splits the check five ways.

  • Table sessions — each table gets its own running bill
  • Multi-bill groups — split checks without the headache
  • Kitchen Display System — live order board, no paper tickets
  • Session policing — expired sessions auto-cancel stale orders
  • QR on every table — no app, no download, just scan
