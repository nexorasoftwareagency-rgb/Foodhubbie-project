The 2026 landscape, by category

Vendors shuffle features and prices every quarter — so cheat-proofed this guide around categories and pricing structures instead of "top 10" rankings that age badly. Named examples are well-known Indian players; verify current pricing with each vendor.

Per-order · all-in-one

1. Per-order systems (like FoodHubbie)

What you get: the whole loop — guest ordering (QR table + WhatsApp), kitchen display, POS counter billing, delivery riders, inventory, campaigns — billed per order, ₹1–5.

Fits: cafes, family restaurants, cloud kitchens and quick-service outlets up to a handful of locations. Deciding check: does the bill follow your volume, or your lease?

Monthly · POS suite

2. Monthly POS suites (e.g. Petpooja, Posist/Restroworks)

What you get: mature counters, deep integrations, big support teams — billed monthly (typically ₹1,500–5,000+, more on enterprise tiers).

Fits: stable high-volume outlets that want one big vendor relationship, and multi-city chains that need payroll/ERP integrations.

Aggregator-dependent

3. Aggregator-only operations

What you get: "management" for free-ish, but every order routes through a 20–30% commission — that's your real software bill, and the customer relationship belongs to the platform.

Fits: nobody who wants to keep margin. If you're here for delivery volume, pair aggregators with a direct channel (WhatsApp + own QR) instead.

The 6-point checklist (works on any vendor)

  • 💸 Price in a slow month — not just a busy one
  • 📱 Guest ordering (QR / WhatsApp) included, or paid add-on?
  • 🛵 Delivery riders managed in-system, or outsourced?
  • 🍳 Does the kitchen get a live screen, not paper KOTs?
  • 🧾 Split bills handled to the paisa?
  • 📊 Do reports export to a CSV your accountant can read?

Buyer questions

Which restaurant management software is best for a small restaurant in India?

For a small single-outlet restaurant the deciding factor is cost structure, not brand. Per-order software like FoodHubbie (₹1–5/order, ₹500 one-time setup, no monthly fee) keeps quiet months cheap; monthly POS suites work when volume is consistently high. Everything else — QR ordering, kitchen display, riders — should be included, not a paid add-on.

Is there free restaurant management software?

Full free plans rarely exist — free tiers are usually demo-limited or ad-supported. The closest to free in real terms is per-order pricing: FoodHubbie's free trial covers every feature with no card, and per-order billing (₹1–5) means you pay roughly nothing in months with few orders.

Monthly fee or per-order — which model is better in 2026?

Per-order wins for most Indian outlets under ~1,000 orders/month because cost tracks revenue. A ₹3,000/month suite costs ₹36,000/year whether or not customers come. Per-order at ₹1–5 costs ₹50–5,000/month depending on your actual business — and both models can be compared live in the free trial.

What features should I check before buying restaurant software?

Check six things: (1) does the price scale down in slow months, (2) is guest-facing ordering (QR/WhatsApp) included or paid, (3) is delivery rider management included, (4) does the kitchen get a live screen, (5) can customers split bills correctly, (6) do reports export to CSV your accountant can read.
