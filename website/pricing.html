Pricing questions

Do I pay for cancelled orders?

No. Orders cancelled before completion are excluded from billing, so a customer who changes their mind does not cost you a per-order fee.

Is there a minimum monthly bill or lock-in?

No minimum and no lock-in. Ten orders in a month means you pay for ten orders, and you can stop using the system without a cancellation fee.

How much does restaurant software cost in India?

It depends on the pricing model. Monthly-fee POS suites charge roughly ₹1,500–5,000 per month. FoodHubbie charges per order instead: ₹1 per counter order, ₹2 per QR table order, ₹5 per WhatsApp order, plus a one-time ₹500 setup — so a typical 500-order month costs ₹1,000–2,500 and a slow month costs almost nothing.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. FoodHubbie has no monthly subscription. You pay a one-time ₹500 setup and then ₹1–5 per order depending on the channel the order came through.

What does the ₹500 setup cover?

The one-time ₹500 covers your outlet onboarding: menu setup help, printed QR codes generated for your tables, admin/staff accounts and WhatsApp bot numbering. It is non-refundable and adjusted in your first bill.

How is the WhatsApp Official rate calculated — ₹5 or 1%?

WhatsApp Official (Meta Cloud API) ordering is billed at the lower of a flat ₹5 per order or 1% of that order's sales value. Official promotional messages are ₹1 each.

Is the free trial really free?

Yes. The trial covers the full system — QR ordering, kitchen display, POS billing, riders, inventory and reports — with no card required. Per-order billing only starts when you go live.
