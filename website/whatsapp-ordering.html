Ordering where the conversation already happens

QR ordering works at the table. WhatsApp ordering works everywhere else: the customer who called yesterday, the office order for twelve, the regular who wants the usual. They send a message, the bot answers with your menu, and the order lands in the same live dashboard as every other channel.

WhatsApp ordering questions, answered plainly

Do customers need to install an app to order on WhatsApp?

No. They message the restaurant's WhatsApp number and the bot replies with the menu. Ordering, customizations and delivery updates all happen in the same chat they already have installed.

Is my WhatsApp number at risk if I send order updates?

Order updates are transactional messages about an order already in progress. On the WhatsApp Official plan they go out through Meta's Cloud API with approved message templates — the route Meta supports for business messaging. On an unofficial connection the software switches promotional sending off, and that number carries the same risk any unofficial WhatsApp connection carries. Either way Meta decides delivery, template approval and account standing: no provider can guarantee a number is never restricted.

Can I send offers and promotions on WhatsApp?

Yes, on the WhatsApp Official plans — campaigns go out as approved marketing templates at ₹1 per message, only to customers who opted in, and STOP is always honoured. Promotional campaigns are disabled on any non-official connection.

Does the customer see delivery status in the chat?

Yes. Order confirmation, preparation and out-for-delivery updates are sent as WhatsApp messages, and the rider's name and phone number reach the customer when the order is assigned.

How is WhatsApp ordering billed?

Orders on the WhatsApp Official plan are ₹5 per order, or 1% of total sales if you prefer the commission model. Promotional messages are ₹1 each, and the one-time setup is ₹500. QR and POS-only plans bill ₹1–2 per order and do not include WhatsApp.
