## The problem (no owner has time for this)

- Orders scribbled on paper, lost in the dinner rush
- Waiters running around tables instead of serving customers
- "Where is my order?" calls from customers and to riders
- No idea which menu items print money and which lose it

## The promise

Every order, every table, every rider, every rupee — visible live from one dashboard.

---

## How it works (3 steps)

1. **Print the QR code.** Customers open your menu on their own phone, customize their pizza, and order without an app or a waiter.
2. **Kitchen sees it instantly.** Orders land on the Kitchen Display Screen — no shouting, no tickets lost.
3. **Riders get dispatched.** Delivery orders auto-assign to the nearest free rider. Customers track them live on a map.

## Features (what's actually inside)

### Customer side (QR ordering)
- No app download — menu opens in the browser
- Customization: toppings, add-ons, sizes — exactly how your kitchen wants it
- Dine-in, takeaway, and delivery from the same menu
- Order tracking with live delivery progress
- Applied discounts and coupons shown transparently before checkout

### Dine-in
- Table management with QR codes on every table
- Table sessions, multi-bill / group billing that stays independent
- Kitchen Display System (KDS) with live order status

### Kitchen & store
- Real-time order board — Pending → Placed → Prepared → Served
- Point of Sale (POS) for walk-ins and phone orders
- Menu & catalog editor with categories, photos, and add-ons
- Inventory with stock levels and CSV import

### Delivery & riders
- Rider app: go online, grab deliveries, get turn-by-turn in-app flow
- Live map tracking of every delivery
- OTP-based handoff so orders reach the right hands
- Rider wallet with earnings, per-outlet breakdown, and settlements

### Marketing & customers
- Promotions engine — compose and send campaigns in-app
- Scheduled discounts and coupon codes
- WhatsApp conversation view with your customers
- Customer records, top customers, and repeat-order insights
- Feedback & reviews management

### Money & analytics
- Dashboard KPIs: orders, revenue, active riders — today, live
- Lost-sales report: orders abandoned at checkout, recovered
- Top items and top customers
- Rider performance & earnings analytics
- Payments and transaction history
- CSV export of sales

### Multi-outlet
- Run several stores (pizza, cake, multiple locations) from one login with per-outlet switching
