## Reddit — helpful replies (never a cold link)

**Thread: "POS recommendation for a small cafe?"**

> What matters more than the POS brand is whether your table orders and your counter bill land in the same place. If they don't, you end up reconciling two systems at close.
>
> Before buying, ask each vendor three things: (1) does a cancelled order get billed, (2) what happens to an order if the internet drops mid-shift, (3) can you export raw order data without asking support. Those three answers separate the tools built for operators from the ones built for demos.

**Thread: "QR ordering worth it?"**

> It pays off when the table is the bottleneck, not the kitchen — busy dine-in with 4+ staff taking orders. Two things to watch: whether the QR is per table (so orders attach to a running bill) or a single menu QR (which just adds a step), and whether guests can call for the bill from the phone. Without those, it's a menu PDF with extra steps.

**Thread: "WhatsApp ordering — is it safe for my number?"**

> The safe version is the official WhatsApp Cloud API with approved templates and opt-in audiences. Bulk sending from an unofficial linked session is what gets numbers restricted. Ask any vendor which transport they use, whether promotions are blocked on unofficial connections, and how opt-outs are handled — if they answer vaguely, that's your answer.
